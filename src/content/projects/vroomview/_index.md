Think, a social media, but specifically for car-people. Enter VroomView. VroomView is a social media app where users can share car concepts, new configurations, etc. then comment or hold discussions. As of today, VroomView is a social media app geared towards car enthusiasts, but if it grows big enough, the long-term aim is to influence car companies through the will of the people in essence, more so “democratizing” the automotive industry.  This app is built with a TypeScript front-end, a database utilizing Supabase, and is a work-in-progress.
